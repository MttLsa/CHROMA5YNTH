Voice, filters and modulation

These modules are shared by all engines (Voice and Modulation tabs; the Filter is the last module of the Engine tab).

Voice

  • Mode: Mono, Poly, Unison, Legato. Max Voices per Layer.
  • Unison 1–16 with Detune, Spread, Pan (Wavetable uses per-oscillator unison).
  • Note Priority: Last, First, Lowest, Highest. Retrigger.
  • Glide: On/Off, 0–5 s, Always/Legato, Linear/Exponential.

Performance

Pitch Bend Range ±1–48, Velocity Curve, Aftertouch Sensitivity, Sustain, Sostenuto, Expression, Mod Wheel Amount.

Filter Library

ZDF/TPT filters with in-loop non-linearity and in-tune self-oscillation. Parameters: On, Model, Cutoff 20 Hz–20 kHz, Resonance, Drive, Key Track, Env Amount (→ Mod Env 1), Velocity, LFO Amount (→ LFO 1). Off by default.

Kind Models
Analog Ladder LP24, Diode LP, SVF LP12/BP12/HP12/Notch, OTA LP24, Cascade LP24, Sallen-Key LP12/HP12, Bridge LP12, Classic LP24
Digital Digital LP12/24/36/48, Digital HP12/24, Digital BP, Digital Notch, Comb +, Comb −, Formant (A-E-I-O-U)

Drift

On, Amount: slow random pitch micro-variations per voice/oscillator (up to ±12 ct) and filter component tolerance (cutoff/resonance differ per voice). Drift Only: filter bypassed, pitch drift active.

Envelopes (ENV)

4 envelopes per Layer: Amp Env and Mod Env 1–3. Attack 0–10 s, Hold 0–10 s, Decay 0–10 s, Sustain 0–100 %, Release 0–20 s, per-segment curve (Linear/Exponential/Logarithmic), Velocity, Key Tracking, Loop, Retrigger, Time Scale, Amount ±100 %.

LFO

4 LFOs per Layer. Shape: Sine, Triangle, Square, Saw Up, Saw Down, S&H, Random Smooth, Custom. Rate 0.01–30 Hz or Sync (1/1…1/32, triplets T, dotted D), Phase 0–360°, Offset, Delay, Fade In/Out, Mode Free/Retrigger/One-shot, Smooth, Stereo Phase, Unipolar/Bipolar, Amount.

Mod Matrix

16 slots per Layer: Source, Destination, Amount ±100 %, Via (source scaling the amount), Curve (Linear, Exponential, Logarithmic, S-Curve), Smoothing, Invert, Enable.

  • Sources: LFO 1–4, Amp Env, Mod Env 1–3, Velocity, Release Velocity, Aftertouch, MPE Pressure/Slide/Pitch, Mod Wheel, Pitch Wheel, Key Track, Note Number, Random (per note), Breath (CC2), Expression (CC11), MIDI CC (any number), Macro 1–8.
  • Destinations: any continuous Layer parameter, including Layer FX (sub-menus by section).

Macros

8 Macros in the Master column, automatable from the host. Right-click a control → assign to Macro → Macro 1–8. A Macro can drive any parameter, also across Layers, and is also a Mod Matrix source.
